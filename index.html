Our Story

Born From a Love of the Wild & Unconventional

Wild Music Group was founded on the belief that the most powerful music comes from artists who refuse to be defined. We exist to champion those artists — to give them the infrastructure, relationships, and creative freedom to build lasting careers on their own terms.

Our roster spans indie and alternative to experimental and world music — united not by genre but by an uncompromising commitment to artistic truth.
